### 3. Dynamic Rendering & Empty States
- Updated `renderExcludedSites()` in [options.ts](file:///x:/Projects_X/0_Active/A_XPI_Firefox_Plugin/firefox-plugin-visit-indicator-xpi_GITHUB/visited-page-tracker/options/options.ts).
